Only the newest rides are kept (10 by default). Download the ones you want to keep. TCX uploads to Strava, Garmin Connect, intervals.icu and most other training sites; CSV opens in a spreadsheet.

Training

FTP (functional threshold power) is the power you can hold for about an hour. It sets the power zones on the dashboard, and IF, TSS and time in zones for each ride. Leave it empty to turn them off. Each ride keeps the FTP it was ridden with.

PIN

With a PIN, nobody else on your Wi-Fi can power off the Pi or change these settings. Forgot it? Remove the "pin" line from settings.json on the Pi.
